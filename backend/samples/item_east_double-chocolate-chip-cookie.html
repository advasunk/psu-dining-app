Penn State makes every effort to label food allergens in the residential dining commons and retail food venues. However, food items are prepared in a shared kitchen; there is a risk for cross contact with major allergens: peanuts, tree nuts, eggs, dairy, soy, wheat/gluten, fish, shellfish and sesame. Guests with food allergies or special dietary restrictions are responsible for being their own advocates and exercising caution while dining.
<br clear="left" /><br />
Fried Foods: Deep fryers are used to prepare foods that contain dairy, eggs, soy, wheat/gluten, fish, and shellfish. Assume all fryers are shared fryers and cross contact cannot be avoided. If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please exercise caution when selecting deep fried foods. Fryers may also interchangeably be used for vegetarian and non-vegetarian items.
<br clear="left" /><br />
Bakery Items: Penn State Bakery items are manufactured in shared production areas in a facility that uses dairy, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, soy, wheat, gluten, and sesame. Please exercise caution when deciding to consume these products if you have a food allergy or sensitivities.
<br clear="left" /><br />
<div>Immediate questions can be directed to a dining services manager, and guests can request information or a meeting with a registered dietitian: <a href="mailto:foodallergies@psu.edu">foodallergies@psu.edu</a>.</div>
